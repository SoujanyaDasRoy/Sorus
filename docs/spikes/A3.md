# Spike A3: DynamicsProcessing fit accuracy

**Verdict: FAIL against "≤ 1 dB max error at 64 bands"; good enough for session mode's everyday curves.**
- **Everyday curves** (shelves, V-shape, Harman-style, headphone correction, tilt, gentle bells) land within about **0.4–1.4 dB** with 40 ms DP frames, or within about **0.6–2.9 dB** with the platform-default frame.
- **Steep filters** (24 dB/oct HP/LP), **deep cuts** (telephone) and **narrow low-frequency features** (50 Hz notch, Q4 at 60 Hz) can't be represented: 6–20 dB off.
- **Ship:** 64 log-spaced bands, last cutoff at Nyquist, band-average fit, platform-default frame (for latency; see "Decisions").

Date: 2026-11-08. Code: `spikes/a3/a3fit.cpp` (desktop), `spikes/a1/.../A3Runner.kt` (on-device). Raw output:
`spikes/a3/results/`.

## Desktop half (flat-band model)

`a3fit.exe` compiles the core's own `response_db` and `fit_to_dp_bands`. It compares 20 reference presets with three fits, each treated as piecewise-flat bands, at 1/48-octave points from 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Targets below −24 dB count as −24.

| Bands | Centre fit (current core) | Band average | Minimax |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | 12 of 20 over 1 dB, worst RMS 1.14 | 12 over, RMS 1.05 | 12 over, RMS 1.30 |
| 64 | 8 over 1 dB, worst RMS 2.17 | **8 over, RMS 0.71** | 8 over, RMS 1.13 |
| 128 | 4 over, RMS 0.73 | 3 over, RMS 0.72 | 3 over, RMS 1.46 |

The failures at 64 bands are always the narrow or steep presets: Q ≥ 3 bells, 24 dB/oct HP, telephone, the hum notch and the 31-band graphic. **The band average beats centre sampling** and becomes the core's fit (M4 Task 1).

## On-device half

The app plays 1/3-octave tones (−20 dBFS) through its own `AudioTrack` session with the fitted DynamicsProcessing pre-EQ. It measures each tone's RMS with a `Visualizer` and compares the response (EQ on minus EQ off) with the target at 31 frequencies.

**Measurement lessons (each one changed the result):**
1. **The phone's speaker path can't be measured.** On the Galaxy A55, the output mix (Visualizer session 0) runs Samsung's SoundAlive tuning, which is level-dependent. Readings scattered ±1.5 dB even where the EQ was flat, and boosts were compressed. A `Visualizer` on the app's own session sits **before** the EQ and sees nothing. So the measurement moved to the **Pixel 8 emulator (Android 17)**, which has stock AOSP audio and the same `DynamicsProcessing` code.
2. **DynamicsProcessing glides after it is enabled** (much longer than 600 ms). Toggling the EQ per tone captured only 40–75 % of each gain. The final method takes one EQ-off reference sweep, then leaves the EQ on and lets it settle per preset.
3. **DP leaves everything above the last band's cutoff untouched.** With the top cutoff at 20 kHz, a 20.5 kHz tone got 0 dB. The last cutoff must be **sample rate / 2**.

**Why the misses happen:**
- **Below about 200 Hz, DP's resolution is coarser than 64 log bands.** A bass boost spreads upward: the +6 dB shelf reads +4.7 at 127 Hz against a target of +1.7. Longer frames (40 ms) halve this error.
- **Deep or steep cuts are only partly realised** (−24 dB targets read about −5 to −17). Steep filters and the telephone preset are out of reach.
- **Notches narrower than the bin width** are impossible.

## Decisions

- **Core:** switch `fit_to_dp_bands` to the band-average fit (M4 Task 1). Callers pass the last cutoff = sample rate / 2.
- **Android session mode:**
  - 64 log bands, `VARIANT_FAVOR_FREQUENCY_RESOLUTION`, **platform-default frame** in v0.
  - 40 ms frames would roughly halve the bass-curve error, but very likely add latency to the mode chosen for video apps (A4). Measure DP's latency per frame duration in M6, then consider 40 ms for non-video apps.
- **UI copy (spec §5, no silent gaps):**
  - Presets with steep HP/LP filters, notches or more than ±12 dB at the extremes get a "session mode can only approximate this curve" note when the app is in session mode.
  - Full mode (M5) runs the real engine and has none of these limits.
- **Not tested:** whether Samsung's DP implementation differs from AOSP. The same `libdynproc` is expected, but the speaker path made a clean measurement impossible. A wired or USB headset might bypass SoundAlive; if one is ever available, re-measure there.
